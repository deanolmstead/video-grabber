Video Grabber.

Paste a link to a web page — or a direct video file — and download the video to this device.

What works, what doesn't

  • Works: direct video files (.mp4, .webm, .mov) and pages that embed a plain video file — news clips, product videos, blog posts, most smaller sites.
  • Won't work: YouTube, Vimeo, Netflix and other streaming services. They never expose a plain video file, so there's nothing here to grab.
  • Some sites block other sites from reading their pages. If that happens, the tool tries a couple of relay services, and as a last resort opens the video so you can save it from your browser (right-click → “Save video as…”).
  • Only download videos you have the right to keep.
Runs entirely in your browser — nothing you paste is stored anywhere.
