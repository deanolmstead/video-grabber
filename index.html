Video Grabber.

Paste a link to a web page — or a direct video file — and download the video to this device.

What works, what doesn't

  • Works: direct video files (.mp4, .webm, .mov), pages that embed a plain video file — news clips, product videos, blog posts, most smaller sites — and streamed videos (HLS / .m3u8), which are downloaded in pieces and joined into a single MP4.
  • Won't work: YouTube, Vimeo, Netflix, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and other streaming/social services, and DRM-encrypted streams. They never expose a plain video file, so there's nothing here to grab.
  • Most sites block other sites from reading their pages, so this tool fetches through a small private proxy first, with several public relay services as backup. If every route is blocked or busy, wait a minute and try again — or paste the video's direct file link, which always works.
  • Some sites lock their video links to the exact session that viewed the page, so no relay can fetch them. For those, each result has a Session download link: it reloads the page and pulls the file in one session, the same trick the big download services use.
  • Only download videos you have the right to keep.
Runs entirely in your browser — nothing you paste is stored anywhere.
