Video Grabber.

Paste a link to a web page — or a direct video file — and download the video to this device.

What works, what doesn't

  • Works: direct video files (.mp4, .webm, .mov) and pages that embed a plain video file — news clips, product videos, blog posts, most smaller sites.
  • Won't work: YouTube, Vimeo, Netflix, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and other streaming/social services. They never expose a plain video file, so there's nothing here to grab.
  • Most sites block other sites from reading their pages, so this tool reads through several public relay services at once and uses whichever gets through. If every relay is blocked or busy, wait a minute and try again — or paste the video's direct file link, which always works.
  • Only download videos you have the right to keep.
Runs entirely in your browser — nothing you paste is stored anywhere.
